# Nightjar, sample `make demo` report

Auto-generated by `make demo` (regenerate: `make demo`). This is the
**reproducible, no-model demo**, a synthetic clip (a person lingers → a
loitering rule fires) replayed through the *full* engine, with Tier-2 as the
deterministic scripted VLM (fixed sim-latency), so any Mac reproduces it in
minutes with no iPhone and no model.

- Device: MacBook M2 Max · commit `8f229fe`
- Tier-2 here: scripted stand-in with sim-latency (encode/prefill/decode are
  *simulated*). Real SmolVLM-500M INT4 numbers (encode ~134 / prefill ~447 /
  decode ~12 ms, Metal encoder) come from `vlm_probe` and the live Mac app -
  see `README.md` and `bench/kleidiai_results.md`. The two are never mixed.
- Harness pumps frames at the clip's wall-clock 30 fps (anti-coordinated-
  omission): when the VLM is busy the ConflatingSlot drops old frames and counts
  them, and the next event's latency is still counted in full.
